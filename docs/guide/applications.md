# Applications

Bottom panel, left. The installed applications as cascading lists.

| List | Contents | Press |
|---|---|---|
| Frequently launched | apps ranked by launches on the current workspace | launch; the button on the right focuses its open window |
| One list per freedesktop category | every other app | launch |

The Launcher group in the [Settings](settings.md) menu hides apps that are already open and sorts categories by use.
