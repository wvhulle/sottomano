# Appearance

The palette icon in the bottom panel opens the looks: ready-made combinations of colour scheme, panel opacity, blur, shadow and outline. Picking one switches the desktop's dark or light mode and saves the new look, so the panel, sidebar and overlay settings can fine-tune it afterwards.

- **Frosted dark**: dark colours, translucent panels frosted with blur.
- **Clear dark**: dark colours, see-through panels without outline, blur or shadow, and a lightly tinted, frosted and outlined sidebar.
- **Bright office**: light colours and nearly opaque panels without blur.

Hover cards stay blurred in every look. The recordings are rendered offscreen, where there is nothing to blur, so blur does not show in them.

## Looks: Switching

Pressing a look applies it at once; the menu marks the one in use.
